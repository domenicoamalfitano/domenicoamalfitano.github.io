# Prof. Domenico Amalfitano

Associate Professor in Computer Engineering  
Department of Electrical Engineering and Information Technologies (DIETI)  
University of Naples Federico II

My research activities cover several key areas in Software Engineering, including software analysis and verification, software testing and test automation, software architecture, and the use of artificial intelligence to support the software lifecycle.

In particular, I work on techniques for analyzing software systems, verifying their correctness, and supporting developers in identifying defects and structural issues. I design methods and tools for generating, executing, and evaluating test suites, with a strong focus on automation to improve efficiency and reliability. 

My research also investigates software architectures, with attention to their quality, evolution, and maintainability. More recently, I have been exploring the use of Large Language Models to support various phases of the software lifecycle, including code refactoring, reverse engineering, and testing automation.
